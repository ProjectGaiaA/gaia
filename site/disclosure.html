Affiliate Disclosure

PlantPriceTracker is a free price comparison tool for garden plants. We check prices at online nurseries every day and show you what each one is charging. This page explains, plainly, how the site is paid for.

How We Make Money

Right now, we don't. PlantPriceTracker is not enrolled in any affiliate or advertising program. No retailer on this site pays us a commission, a referral fee, or anything else, and none of the links on this site are affiliate links.

We do intend to apply to affiliate programs so the site can pay for itself. If we are accepted into one, this page, the notice in the footer of every page, and the notice above every price table will all change to say so before a single paid link goes live.

Status last reviewed: August 2026.

What This Means for You

  • You never pay more because of us. We never add a fee or a markup to a retailer's price — the prices shown are the retailer's own. An affiliate link, if and when we use one, does not change what you are charged; any commission comes out of the retailer's marketing budget.
  • We track nurseries that could never pay us. 2 of the retailers listed below run no affiliate program at all — there is no commission to earn from them under any circumstances, and we track them anyway. Showing you the genuinely cheapest option matters more to us than what we might earn on a click.
  • Rankings are based on price, and only price. The lowest price is highlighted because it is the lowest price. No retailer can buy placement, ordering, or a recommendation on this site.
