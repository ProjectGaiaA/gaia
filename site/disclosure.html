Affiliate Disclosure

PlantPriceTracker is a free price comparison tool for garden plants. We check prices at online nurseries every day and show you what each one is charging. This page explains, plainly, how the site is paid for.

How We Make Money

Some of the links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, the retailer may pay us a commission at no extra cost to you. That is the intended way for this site to cover its own costs.

Being specific about the scale of it, because "some links" could mean anything: exactly one link on this entire site is an affiliate link today, out of an outbound link on every retailer row of every plant page. Applications to further programs are in review and have not been approved. No affiliate program has paid us anything to date. As more links become affiliate links, that number goes up on its own, because it is counted from the links themselves rather than typed here by hand.

Every affiliate link is marked with an asterisk (*) in the comparison table on the page it appears on, and carries rel="sponsored" in its markup. Every other link on this site is an ordinary link that earns us nothing.

Status last reviewed: August 2026.

What This Means for You

  • You never pay more because of us. We never add a fee or a markup to a retailer's price. The prices shown are the retailer's own. An affiliate link does not change what you are charged; any commission comes out of the retailer's marketing budget.
  • Monetization cannot move the comparison. Which retailers appear, what prices they show, and which price is highlighted as cheapest are all computed from the prices we scrape. That calculation never reads the affiliate data at all. An affiliate link changes the destination of one link and nothing else.
  • We track nurseries we earn nothing from. That is nearly all of them: 7 nurseries tracked, one affiliate link. Showing you the genuinely cheapest option matters more to us than what we might earn on a click.
  • Rankings are based on price, and only price. The lowest price is highlighted because it is the lowest price. No retailer can buy placement, ordering, or a recommendation on this site, and commission rates play no part in how prices are sorted.
