About PlantPriceTrackerBot

This page is for site owners who see our bot in their logs. If you are here because of something our bot did, the short version is: tell us and we will stop.

Who we are

PlantPriceTracker is a small, independently run price comparison site for garden plants. We show what the same plant costs at several nurseries side by side, and every listing links back to the nursery so the visit and the sale go to you.

What the bot does

  • It reads the price, the size options and whether an item is in stock, on product pages for roughly 100 plants we track.
  • It runs twice a day and requests only pages for products we already track — on the order of a few dozen requests per nursery per day, with a randomised pause of 5 to 15 seconds between requests.
  • It checks robots.txt before requesting a page and does not request anything disallowed for User-agent: *.

What it never does

  • It never creates an account, signs in, or submits a form.
  • It never adds to a cart or begins a checkout.
  • It never solves or works around a CAPTCHA, and it does not use proxies or rotate IP addresses.
  • It does not copy product descriptions, photographs or written content. What we store per product is a price, a size label, a stock flag, a timestamp and a link back to you.

If you want us to stop

You can block us in robots.txt and we will honour it:

User-agent: PlantPriceTrackerBot
Disallow: /

Or write to us and we will remove your store, including any prices already collected: contact us here. We would rather hear from you than be blocked, and if the problem is how often we visit or which pages we request, we will change it.
