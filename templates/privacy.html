What We Collect

PlantPriceTracker is a static website. There are no accounts, no logins, and no forms on this site. We do not ask you for your name, email address, postal address, or payment information, and there is nowhere on the site to give them to us.

There is no mailing list, no newsletter, and no price-alert subscription. If you want to hear from us, you email us; we have no way to email you first.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics, and nothing else. It is a small script that runs in your browser and reports one anonymous record per page view to Vercel, our host.

That record contains the page you viewed, the referring site if there was one, and coarse details derived from your request: country, operating system, browser, and device type. It does not contain your name, your email address, or your IP address (Vercel discards it after deriving country).

Vercel Web Analytics sets no cookies and assigns no persistent identifier, so it cannot follow you from one visit to the next or from this site to any other site. Vercel's own analytics privacy documentation describes exactly what it stores.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook or Meta pixels, advertising trackers, session recorders, heat-map tools, or any other analytics product.

Cookies and Local Storage

We set no cookies. None. There is no cookie banner on this site because there is nothing to consent to.

We do use localStorage, a browser feature that stores data on your own device, to remember two things: the plants you save to My List, and your growing zone on the heat map. That data never leaves your browser and is never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Affiliate Links

Some outbound links on this site are affiliate links. When you click one, the retailer or the affiliate network handling the link may set their own cookies to attribute the referral. That happens on their side, under their privacy policy, and we have no access to it and no control over it. See our affiliate disclosure for which links those are and how the arrangement works.

Every outbound link on this site, affiliate or not, goes to a third-party retailer whose own privacy policy governs what happens once you arrive.

Hosting

This site is hosted on Vercel, which processes the ordinary web-server request data any host receives in order to serve you a page. Vercel's privacy policy applies to that infrastructure.

Your Data

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to show you, correct, or delete. If you have emailed us, we hold that email in our mailbox; ask and we will delete it.

You can clear the data this site stored on your own device by clearing your browser's site data for this domain.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and since it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If we change how this site handles data, we will change this page in the same release and update the date at the top.
