Help Us Build a Better Tracker

This site is built by one person. If a price is wrong, a plant is missing, or something is broken, telling us is the fastest way to get it fixed.

Email {{ contact_email }}

What's Worth Sending

  • A wrong price. Send the plant page, the nursery, and what the nursery's own site says. Prices come from automated daily checks, and a scraper that has drifted stays wrong until somebody notices it.
  • A plant or variety we don't track. The cultivar name is enough. If you know which nurseries sell it, better.
  • A nursery we should be tracking. Independent and regional sellers especially.
  • Something broken. A page that won't load, a link to nowhere, a table that breaks on your phone. The page address and your browser are enough.
  • A feature you want. Say what you were trying to do and where the site got in your way. That is more useful than the feature name.

Every message goes to a real, monitored mailbox: {{ contact_email }}. See the contact page for what we can and can't help with.

What We're Working On

This is our own plan, in the order we intend to do it. It is not a vote count and nobody has been promised a date.

More nurseries

We currently track {{ retailer_count }}. Adding regional and specialty sellers is the single change that improves every comparison on the site at once, so it stays at the top.

More plants and varieties

Coverage is broad on shrubs and trees and thin on perennials. Filling that in is steady background work.

Bulb price comparison

Nobody is doing real price comparison on tulips, daffodils, alliums, and crocus across suppliers. Fall bulb season is the target.

Local nursery pricing

Starting with Bucks County, Pennsylvania, because that is where we can physically check the prices, then expanding.

Better price history

We keep every price we have ever recorded. Surfacing more of it, so you can see whether today's price is actually good, is worth more than any new feature.

What We Won't Do

  • Rank by commission. The cheapest price is highlighted because it is the cheapest. No retailer can buy placement, ordering, or a recommendation. See the disclosure.
  • Hide a nursery that pays us nothing. Most of them pay us nothing. They are all in the table.
  • Invent activity. If this page ever shows what other readers asked for, it will be because they actually asked.
